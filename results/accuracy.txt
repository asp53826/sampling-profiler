  Mean error tracks the binomial standard error, which is the whole
  behaviour of a sampling profiler: it is an estimator, and its noise
  is predictable rather than mysterious.

=== the floor: when is a hot path distinguishable from nothing? ===

  A function holding share p is 'detected' when its measured share is
  more than two standard errors above zero — the usual bar for saying
  a count is not noise. Solving 2·sqrt(p(1-p)/n) < p gives n > 4(1-p)/p.
